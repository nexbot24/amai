About AMAI

A luxury space for beauty beyond the treatment.

AMAI was created to offer thoughtful beauty and body treatments in a calm, luxurious environment. While specialising in professional waxing, our vision extends beyond a single service to encompass beauty, skin, body and wellness experiences that support confidence, comfort and self-care.

Care,
unhurried.
What we believe

We believe self-care is not only about outward appearance, but also about confidence, comfort and feeling good within yourself. AMAI's approach is centred around making you feel well cared for, comfortable and confident in your own skin.

How we work

At AMAI, every treatment is carried out with purpose, attention to detail and respect for the individual.

The vision

More than a treatment. A brand of self-care.

AMAI begins with professional waxing — precise, private, discreet — and expands into skin, body and intimate care. Each treatment is designed with the same care as the first appointment.

Our philosophy

A slower, more considered approach.

At AMAI, we believe self-care is about more than how you look. While our treatments are designed to help you feel confident on the outside, we place equal importance on how you feel within yourself.

From the way you are welcomed to the care, privacy and attention you receive throughout your treatment, everything at AMAI is considered to help you leave feeling comfortable, confident and genuinely good in yourself.
